06 — Spacing
Clear Space &
Minimum Size
To maintain the integrity and impact of the Peramio logo, a minimum clear space must be maintained around it at all times. The clear space is defined by the height of the letter "o" in the logotype (referred to as "X").
The clear space "X" equals the height of the lowercase "o" in the logotype. No graphic elements, text, or edges should enter this protected zone.
Minimum Sizes
Digital
Min. 80px
Print
Min. 25mm
Recommended
120px+ / 40mm+
